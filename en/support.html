Send a message to appsbeheerder@lichtgolf.nl. Please mention which device you use, which version of the app (shown at the bottom of the settings) and what exactly went wrong.

Frequently asked questions

The app does not scan my code

Check whether it is a format that can be read — see the list on the home page. Make sure there is enough light, hold the device steady, and fill the viewfinder with the code without getting too close.

I created a code but the app will not read it back

Seven formats can be created but not read: EAN-2, EAN-5, GS1-128, Telepen and RM4SCC, plus ISBN and ITF-14 — those last two are read, but the app calls them EAN-13 and ITF, because technically that is what they are. The app warns you about this when you pick the format.

Why does the app add a digit to my EAN?

That is the check digit. An EAN-13 consists of twelve digits plus a thirteenth calculated from the other twelve. If you type it yourself, the app verifies it instead of adding one.

Where is my history stored?

Encrypted on your own device, in the app's private folder. Nothing goes to a server. You can delete entries individually or all at once.

Can I get my history back after "delete all"?

No. That action also replaces the key, precisely so that nothing can be recovered. That is why it asks for confirmation first.

The ticket vault asks for my fingerprint every single time

That is by design. The vault's key is released only after authentication and is not kept in memory afterwards. If the app held on to it, the lock would be no more than a screen in front of the door.

The app says the ticket vault is unavailable

Two things have to be in place: a screen lock on the device (PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint) and Android 10 or later. If either is missing, the app creates no vault — calling something a vault while the door stands open serves no purpose.

My tickets will not open after a new fingerprint was added

When a fingerprint or face scan is added to the device, Android discards the vault's key. That is deliberate: otherwise someone who knows your PIN could add a finger of their own and reach your tickets. What was in the vault becomes unreadable; only a backup brings it back. The app warns you about this before you start using the vault.

What is in the paid version?

The ticket vault, keeping a code as SVG or PDF, a PNG at high resolution, and reading a code from a shared PDF. Everything else is free. It is a single one-off purchase inside the app; there is no advertising and no subscription. The full list is on the home page.

I lost the password to my backup

Then that file can no longer be opened, not even by the app's author. The password is not stored anywhere; there is nothing to recover or reset. That is exactly what makes the backup worth something.
